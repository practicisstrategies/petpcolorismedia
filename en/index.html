How It Works

From your file to your parts.

01
Send Your File

Send us your 2D file (DXF, SVG, AI). Cut lines must be pure red (RGB 255, 0, 0) and closed.

02
Review & Quote

We review your geometry and send back a quote with lead times. Prototypes in 48 hours, production runs in 10 business days.

03
Precision Cutting

CNC laser cutting with tolerance verification. Clean, sealed edges, no fraying — even on Nomex 410 and delicate materials.

04
Inspection & Delivery

Optical or probe quality control. Your parts are packaged and shipped — or available for pickup in Montreal.
