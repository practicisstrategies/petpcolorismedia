Materials / Hitex 020 Grey
Thermosetting Insulation · Dielectric Rigidity

Hitex 020 Grey for thermal and mechanical insulation.

Dimensional stability, low water absorption, and dielectric rigidity of 10–14 kV/mm. Precision laser cutting for industrial electrical and mechanical components.

Overview

The rigid insulator of choice for demanding environments.

Hitex 020 Grey is a thermosetting insulating material introduced in the 1980s. Made from blended fibers impregnated with a phenolic resin, processed under pressure and heat, it produces a robust, stable material.

Its superior mechanical properties remain consistent under varying environmental conditions. Its very low water absorption and good dielectric rigidity make it a top-tier choice for electrical and mechanical insulation parts where dimensional precision is critical. P&P Colorismédia can source it in various shapes and thicknesses for your project.

Properties

Technical characteristics of Hitex 020 Grey.

Dimensional Stability

Stable shape under mechanical stress and thermal variation. Tight tolerances maintained throughout the part's service life.

Moisture Resistance

Water absorption below 0.3%. Retains its electrical and mechanical properties in humid environments.

Dielectric Rigidity

10 to 14 kV/mm depending on thickness. Provides reliable electrical insulation for transformers, motors, and high-voltage equipment.

Mechanical Strength

Excellent compression and abrasion resistance. Supports structural loads in demanding industrial equipment.
