About

Specialized expertise serving insulating materials since 2009.

Founded in Montreal, P&P Colorismédia supports the most demanding industrial projects with precision laser cutting, direct hands-on service, and proven know-how on materials nobody else masters as well.

Our Story

Born from a conviction: industry deserves better than a generalist shop.

Founded in Montreal in 2009, P&P Colorismédia specialized from day one in what few laser services dare to tackle: the most demanding insulating materials in the electrical and thermal industries.

Aramid Nomex, phenolic Hitex, cellular neoprene, high-temperature silicone — these materials demand fine control of laser power, cutting speed, and environmental parameters to produce clean, precise, repeatable parts. This expertise cannot be improvised.

For over fifteen years, we've served electrical equipment manufacturers, defense engineers, motor and transformer builders who need a laser-cutting partner who knows their materials as well as they do — and who answers the phone.

Our team handles every order directly, with no sales intermediaries. From the initial DXF file to the packaged part, every step is followed by technicians who know your project personally.
