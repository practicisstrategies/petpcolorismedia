Materials / High-Temperature Silicone
Synthetic Polymer · Thermal Insulation

Silicone for flexible, heat-resistant parts.

Thermal resistance up to 220°C, excellent flexibility, and good insulating properties. Precision laser cutting, no minimum order.

Overview

Thermal flexibility for the hottest applications.

Silicone is a synthetic polymer made of silicon, oxygen, carbon, and hydrogen. It's known for its exceptional flexibility, heat resistance, and remarkable insulating properties.

Resistant to moisture, oils, solvents, and extreme temperatures, silicone is ideal for insulation and sealing applications requiring both flexibility and thermal stability. Non-toxic and biocompatible in certain applications, it fits a wide range of industrial sectors. P&P Colorismédia laser-cuts it with precision for your custom parts.

Properties

Technical characteristics of Silicone.

Thermal Resistance

Continuous service from -60°C to +220°C. Retains its elastic and insulating properties across this entire temperature range.

Superior Flexibility

Excellent flexibility and elasticity maintained at both high and low temperatures. Ideal for parts subjected to repeated deformation.

Electrical Insulation

Good dielectric properties up to 20 kV/mm. Used as an insulator in electronics and high-voltage equipment.

Chemical Resistance

Stable against moisture, oils, solvents, and chemicals. Low water absorption and good dimensional stability.
