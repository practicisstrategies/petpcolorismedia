Materials / Mylar®
Biaxially Oriented PET Film · Fine Insulation

Mylar® — polyester film for precision insulation.

Ultra-thin PET film, dimensionally stable and moisture resistant. Laser-cut with exceptional precision for capacitors, flat cables, and electronic components.

Overview

The reference insulating film for fine electronics.

Mylar® is a biaxially oriented polyethylene terephthalate (PET) film produced by DuPont Teijin Films. This orientation process gives the film exceptional dimensional stability, high mechanical strength, and low gas and moisture permeability.

Available in thicknesses from 6 to 500 µm, Mylar® fits the most demanding applications in electronics, industrial packaging, and electrical insulation. P&P Colorismédia laser-cuts it with precision down to ±0.1 mm for clean, tear-free complex parts.

Properties

Technical characteristics of Mylar®.

Dimensional Stability

Biaxial orientation ensuring resistance to thermal and mechanical deformation. Ideal for tight tolerances in electronic assembly.

High Dielectric Strength

100 to 300 V/µm depending on thickness. Provides reliable insulation in capacitors, flat cables, and PCB insulators.

Low Moisture Absorption

Under 0.3% water absorption — stable electrical properties even in humid environments or under temperature variations.

Chemical Resistance

Good resistance to dilute acids, hydrocarbons, and common solvents. Resists surface abrasion without loss of insulating properties.

Applications

  • 02Flat cable (FFC/FPC) insulation and interconnect ribbons
  • 03Insulating barriers in transformers and inductors
  • 04Insulating interleaves and coatings in battery assemblies
  • 05Protective films for sensitive electronic components
