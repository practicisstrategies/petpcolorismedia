Materials / TPU
Thermoplastic Polyurethane · Precision Elastomer

TPU — flexible, abrasion-resistant elastomer.

Thermoplastic polyurethane combines the flexibility of rubber with the strength of engineering plastics. Laser-cut for flexible gaskets, abrasion protection, and custom parts.

Overview

The versatility of rubber with the precision of thermoplastic.

TPU (thermoplastic polyurethane) is an elastomer that occupies a unique position between rubbers and rigid plastics. It combines flexibility and elasticity comparable to rubber with abrasion, oil, and fuel resistance superior to most conventional elastomers.

Available in hardnesses from 60 Shore A to 70 Shore D, TPU fits a wide range of applications — seals, abrasion protection, flexible gaskets, and technical parts. P&P Colorismédia laser-cuts it with precision for clean edges and complex shapes without tearing.

Properties

Technical characteristics of TPU.

Abrasion Resistance

Among the best abrasion resistance of any elastomer — ideal for parts subjected to repeated friction or abrasive surfaces.

Oil and Fuel Resistance

Excellent resistance to mineral oils, fuels, greases, and many solvents. Outperforms neoprene on this point for oil-industry applications.

Elasticity and Low Compression Set

High rebound and low permanent deformation under load. TPU seals retain their tightness over long service periods.

Wide Hardness Range

From 60 Shore A (very soft) to 70 Shore D (semi-rigid) — a single material family covers a spectrum of applications unique among elastomers.
