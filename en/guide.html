Design Rules

Six rules for a successful cut, first time.

Format
Vector
Vector files only

Send your files in PDF, DXF, AI, EPS, or DWG. Raster images (JPG, PNG) cannot be cut directly — all outlines must be vector paths.

Bridges & Ribs
≥ 0.8 mm
Minimum bridge width

Any bridge, rib, or wall between two cuts must measure at least 0.8 mm. Below that value, fragile parts risk warping or breaking during removal.

Holes & Piercings
≥ thickness
Minimum hole diameter

A hole's diameter must be at least equal to the material thickness, with an absolute minimum of 0.5 mm. Holes that are too small cannot be cut cleanly and risk burning.

Margins
≥ 3 mm
Margin from the edges

Keep a margin of at least 3 mm between the edge of your part and the edges of the material sheet. Cuts too close to the edge can produce irregularities or burning.

Text & Engraving
≥ 3 mm
Minimum character height

For legible engraving or marking, use a minimum height of 3 mm per character. Avoid light or very thin fonts whose strokes would be under 0.3 mm.

Scale
1:1
Actual-scale file

Always send your file at 1:1 scale (actual size). Specify the units (mm or inches). An incorrectly scaled file is the most frequent and costly source of error.

Summary

Checklist before sending your file.

Do
  • ✓ Vector files — PDF, DXF, AI, EPS, or DWG
  • ✓ Closed contours and clean paths, no floating points
  • ✓ 1:1 scale with clearly specified units (mm or in)
  • ✓ Separate layers for cut lines, engraving, and construction
  • ✓ Bridges ≥ 0.8 mm and holes ≥ material thickness
  • ✓ Margin ≥ 3 mm from the sheet edges
  • ✓ Specify the material, thickness, and desired quantity
Don't
  • ✕ Raster images — JPG, PNG, BMP, TIFF not accepted as cut paths
  • ✕ Double or overlapping lines — they produce duplicate laser passes
  • ✕ Construction geometry not removed from the final file
  • ✕ Holes smaller than 0.5 mm or than the material thickness
  • ✕ Margin under 3 mm from the sheet edges
  • ✕ File with no indication of units or scale
  • ✕ Open contours — an unclosed path results in an incomplete cut
