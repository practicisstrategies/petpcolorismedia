Privacy Policy.

How 2009 P&P Colorismedia Inc. collects, uses and protects your personal information.

Language: English · Français

Effective August 15, 2026

1. Purpose

This policy describes how we collect, use and protect your personal information in the course of our business.

2. Personal Information Collected

We may collect, in particular: last name, first name, address, email address, telephone number, billing information, information related to service requests and any information provided voluntarily.

3. Collection methods

Information is collected through website forms, emails, telephone, service requests, submissions and site interactions.

4. Use of Information

The information is used to respond to requests, provide services, administer business relationships, improve the site and comply with legal obligations.

5. Consent

By submitting your personal information, you consent to its collection, use and disclosure for the purposes described in this policy.

6. Disclosure to third parties

We do not sell or rent personal information. It may be shared with authorized service providers when necessary for the provision of the services or when required by law.

7. Protection of personal information

Reasonable administrative, technical, and physical measures are in place to protect the information from unauthorized access, use, modification, or disclosure.

8. Retention and Destruction

Information is retained only for as long as necessary for the purposes for which it was collected or to comply with legal obligations. It is then destroyed or anonymized in a secure manner.

9. Your rights

You may request access to, rectification of, or withdrawal of your consent where permitted by law.

10. Privacy Incidents

In the event of an incident that poses a risk of serious harm, the company will apply the measures required by law, including notifying affected persons and competent authorities, when required.
