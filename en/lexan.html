Materials / Lexan®
Polycarbonate · Guards & Panels

Lexan® — high-impact transparent polycarbonate.

Transparent polycarbonate offering impact resistance up to 250 times greater than glass. Laser- or CNC-cut for guards, covers, and industrial control panels.

Overview

The strength of engineering plastic with the clarity of glass.

Lexan® is a high-performance polycarbonate brand developed by SABIC. Its exceptional impact resistance, combined with high optical clarity and good thermal performance, makes it the ideal glass substitute in industrial applications.

Available in sheets from 1 to 12 mm and thicker, Lexan® is laser-cut for complex shapes and CNC-cut for heavier gauges. P&P Colorismédia produces guards, visors, panels, and structural parts with clean edges and precise tolerances.

Properties

Technical characteristics of Lexan®.

Exceptional Impact Resistance

250 times more impact-resistant than glass at equivalent thickness. Doesn't shatter into dangerous shards — maximum safety in industrial environments.

Optical Clarity

88% light transmission — nearly identical to glass. Also available in tinted, opaque, or smoked finishes depending on the application.

Thermal Stability

Continuous service from −40°C to +120°C. Maintains its rigidity and optical properties across the full industrial service temperature range.

Relative Lightness

About half the weight of glass at equivalent dimensions. Eases installation and reduces the structural load of covers and guards.
