Our Services

Three cutting methods, one shared expertise.

Die-cutting, CNC, or laser — we select the optimal method for each project based on material, complexity, and production volume.

Cutting Processes

The right process for every application.

Each cutting method has its advantages depending on the material, thickness, part complexity, and required volume. Our team advises you on the process best suited to your project.

01 — High Volume
Die-Cutting

Die-cutting uses a precision steel die to stamp shapes directly into the sheet material. No heat is generated — ideal for series production requiring perfect repeatability.

  • No heat-affected zone — clean edges with no burning
  • High throughput for large production runs
  • Perfect repeatability, part after part
  • Suited to simple to moderately complex shapes
Request a Quote
02 — Thick Materials
CNC Cutting

CNC (computer numerical control) cutting uses computer-guided rotary cutting tools. Versatile and precise, it excels on rigid, thick materials where laser cutting or die-cutting reach their limits.

  • Rigid, thick materials up to several centimeters
  • Complex 2D shapes with no dedicated tooling
  • Clean finishes on hard materials
  • Ideal for structural parts and prototypes
Request a Quote
03 — Ultra-Precision
Laser Cutting

Our flagship process. Laser cutting uses a high-intensity beam to cut materials with precision down to ±0.1 mm. No physical contact with the part — clean, sealed edges, ideal for delicate materials and the most complex shapes.

  • Tolerances down to ±0.1 mm
  • Clean edges, no fraying or burrs
  • No tooling — economical from the first part
  • Nomex, silicone, neoprene, Hitex, and more
