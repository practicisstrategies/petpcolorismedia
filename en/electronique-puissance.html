Industries / Power Electronics
Thermal & Power · Servers · Telecom

Custom thermal insulation for power electronics.

Heat-dissipation gaskets, thermal barriers, and insulating parts for power transistors, inverters, and converters — cut in silicone and polyimide film with tenth-of-a-millimeter precision.

Sector Expertise

Thermal management starts with a well-cut part.

In power electronics, heat is enemy number one. A poorly fitted thermal gasket — too big, too small, wrong thickness — compromises contact between the component and the heat sink. Precision laser cutting eliminates that guesswork.

We supply manufacturers of servers, telecommunications equipment, industrial power supplies, and variable-speed drives. Our flexible silicone and Kapton® film parts are cut to ±0.1 mm tolerances — exact dimensions for optimal thermal contact and electrical insulation.

Key Advantages

Why power electronics manufacturers choose P&P.

Perfect Fit to the Component

A thermal gasket cut to the exact dimensions of the transistor or IGBT module maximizes contact area — minimized thermal resistance, extended service life.

Guaranteed Electrical Isolation

Silicone and Kapton® provide high electrical insulation while conducting heat. No accidental short between the component case and the grounded heat sink.

Service-Temperature Resistance

Silicone stable from −60°C to +220°C, Kapton® up to +400°C — parts hold up in compact, high-power-density enclosures without degrading.

Fast Prototypes for R&D

No tooling cost — ideal for design iterations. Validate your gasket geometry in 48 h before launching a production run.
