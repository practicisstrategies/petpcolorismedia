Materials / Nomex 410
Aramid Paper · Electrical Insulation

Nomex 410 for thermal and electrical insulation.

Dielectric strength up to 500 V/mil, UL 94V-0 certified. Precision laser cutting, no minimum order, fast delivery from Montreal.

Overview

The reference aramid paper for the electrical industry.

Nomex 410 is a thermosetting aramid paper introduced in the 1960s. Made from resin-impregnated aramid fibers, processed under pressure and heat, it produces a strong, stable, flame-retardant material.

It meets UL 94V-0 standards and MIL-I-24768/28 GEB-F requirements. Its dielectric strength — the best of all the materials we work with — makes it essential for high-performance electrical applications. P&P Colorismédia can source it in various thicknesses and configurations to fit your project.

Key Selling Point

Class R thermal resistance (220°C) and flame retardant.

Properties

Technical characteristics of Nomex 410.

Thermal Resistance

Continuous service up to 220°C. UL 94V-0 certified, it retains its properties in hot and variable environments.

Dielectric Strength

Up to 500 V/mil, the best dielectric strength among all our materials. Ideal for high-voltage insulation.

Mechanical Strength

70,000 PSI flexural strength. Good impact, adhesion, and tear resistance for demanding technical parts.

Low Water Absorption

Stable electrical properties in humid environments and variable temperatures. Low dissipation factor.
