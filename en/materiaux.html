Flexible Insulation

Nomex 410

Thermosetting Aramid Paper · Beige

The electrical industry standard since the 1960s. Made from aramid fibers, it offers the best dielectric strength of all our materials — 500 V/mil — while withstanding temperatures up to 220°C. UL 94V-0 certified and MIL-I-24768/28 compliant.

Class R thermal resistance (220°C) and flame retardant.Transformer and electric vehicle manufacturers

Rigid Insulation

Hitex 020 Grey

Phenolic Thermosetting · Grey

A thermosetting structural insulator manufactured under pressure and heat from phenolic fibers. Its very low water absorption (< 0.3%) and stable mechanical properties make it the reference choice for rigid insulation parts requiring dimensional precision and long-term durability.

Mechanical rigidity and cost-effective insulation.Coil winding shops and motor manufacturers

Dielectric 10–14 kV/mm

Gaskets & Sealing

Neoprene CloseCells

Synthetic Elastomer · Black

A synthetic rubber known for its versatile resistance to fluids, ozone, chemicals, and heat. Its low compression set and high resilience make it the material of choice for gaskets subjected to constant pressure and repeated cycling.

Service temp. -40 → +120°C

High-Temperature Silicone

Silicone Polymer · Red or Clear

A synthetic polymer combining extreme flexibility with thermal resistance up to 220°C. Non-toxic and biocompatible in certain applications, it excels in demanding industrial environments where a part must remain flexible under continuous heat while providing electrical insulation and sealing.
