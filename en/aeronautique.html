Industries / Aerospace & Defense
High Performance · Strict Certifications

High-performance insulation parts for aerospace and defense.

Insulation for complex wire harnesses, gaskets for black boxes, and radar components in Nomex and silicone — lightweight, flame-resistant materials that meet aerospace certification tolerances.

Sector Expertise

Where weight and reliability are non-negotiable.

Aerospace and defense impose some of the strictest quality standards in industry. Every gram saved has value. Every part must perform without failure under extreme conditions — vibration, thermal shock, exposure to flame and hydraulic fluids.

Nomex® is the aerospace industry's reference material for its unmatched flame-resistance-to-weight ratio. Certified to FAR 25.853 and compliant with mil-spec standards, it's laser-cut with the precision required by critical applications. Silicone completes the range for gaskets in vibrating environments.

Key Advantages

Why aerospace players choose P&P.

Certified Flame Resistance

Nomex 410 is UL 94V-0 certified and compliant with FAR 25.853 requirements for aircraft interior applications. Doesn't burn, doesn't drip, self-extinguishes immediately.

Maximum Weight Savings

Nomex offers high dielectric and mechanical strength at very low weight. In aerospace, lightening every component is both an economic and operational necessity.

Vibration and Shock Resistance

Silicone parts absorb structural vibration without losing their seal. Nomex maintains its mechanical properties through the thermal-shock cycles of flight.

Traceability and Repeatability

Every part is produced by numerical control from your certified file. Repeatability is total — essential for certification records and quality audits.
