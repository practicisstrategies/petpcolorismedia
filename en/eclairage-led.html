Industries / Industrial LED Lighting
Industrial Lighting · LED Thermal Management

Custom insulating components for industrial LED lighting.

Insulating gaskets for LED drivers, thermal barriers between circuit and housing, and interface pads — in thin silicone and insulating films laser-cut to maximize the life of your fixtures.

Sector Expertise

Heat is enemy number one for LED lifespan.

LED junction temperature directly determines lifespan and lumen maintenance. A poorly fitted thermal gasket or insulation barrier compromises heat dissipation and accelerates aging. Precision laser cutting eliminates those variables.

We supply manufacturers of industrial LED fixtures, illuminated signage, and outdoor lighting. Our flexible silicone, Mylar®, and Kapton® parts are cut to the exact dimensions of every driver and heat sink — no approximate hand-cutting, no misaligned gasket.

Key Advantages

Why LED fixture manufacturers choose P&P.

Optimal Thermal Management

A silicone gasket cut to the driver's exact dimensions maximizes contact with the heat sink. Minimized thermal resistance, lower junction temperature, extended lifespan.

Reliable Electrical Isolation

Mylar® and Kapton® films electrically isolate the driver circuit from its grounded metal housing. Prevents current leakage and meets fixture safety standards.

IP65–IP67 Sealing

Neoprene and silicone gaskets cut to the fixture's exact profile guarantee protection against dust and water ingress — essential for industrial environments.

Fast Design Iterations

No tooling — every shape revision is produced from the new DXF. Validate your thermal assembly in 48 h before launching production.
