Overview

A general-purpose elastomer for the most demanding applications.

Neoprene is a synthetic rubber foam produced by the polymerization of chloroprene. It's a general-purpose elastomer known for its resistance to flex cracking, low compression set, and high resilience.

Neoprene resists moisture, oil, solvents, oxygen, chemicals, ozone, heat, and grease. These properties make it the material of choice for sealing applications where most other materials would fall short. P&P Colorismédia laser-cuts it with precision for your gaskets, seals, and flexible insulators.

Properties

Technical characteristics of Neoprene.

Fluid Resistance

Resists oil, water, solvents, ozone, and chemicals. Ideal for humid or chemical industrial environments.

Low Compression Set

Holds its shape under sustained load. A key property for gaskets subjected to constant pressure.

High Resilience

Good abrasion and flex-cracking resistance. Withstands repeated compression and vibration cycles without degrading.

Wide Temperature Range

Stable service from -40°C to +120°C depending on the application. Maintains sealing properties across the full temperature range.
